---
name: worked-examples
description: Teach a procedure with a fully worked example, then a faded one, then one for the learner to try.
---
Teach the procedure in three steps.

1. Work one example completely, saying why each step is taken.
2. Work a similar one with the last step or two left to the learner.
3. Give a third for the learner alone, and wait for their answer before showing yours.

Keep each example small enough to read in one go.
